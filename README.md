### 1. What is a Custom Audience & Why is it the Secret Weapon for High ROI?
* **Core Concept:** Cold audience se conversion rate kam hota hai (1%-2%), isliye un logo ko retarget kiya jata hai jinhone pehle aapke business ke sath koi interaction kiya ho (Warm / Hot Audience)[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
* **The 2 Broad Categories:** 
  1. *Your Sources:* Website (Pixel), Customer List (CSV upload), App Activity, aur Offline Activity[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
  2. *Meta Sources:* Video views, Instagram Business Profile interaction, Lead Form engagement, Facebook Page interactions, Events, aur Shopping[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

### 2. Website Pixel & Customer List Uploads
* **Website Custom Audience:** Meta Pixel ki madad se aap alag-alag rules set kar sakte hain jaise All Website Visitors (30/90/180 days), Specific Web Pages, Visitors by time spent, aur Add to Cart vs Purchased (Cart Abandoners)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
* **Customer List Uploads & Exclusion Strategy:** Apne existing clients ya students ke Phone Numbers aur Email IDs ki CSV file upload karke Custom Audience banai jati hai[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Purane students ko exclude karne se ad budget waste nahi hota aur Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) kam hoti hai[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

### 3. Video Engagement & Watch Time Thresholds
* **Watch Time Intent Levels:**
  * *3-Second / 10-Second Views (ThruPlay):* Casual scrollers, Low intent (Cold - Mild Warm)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).

### 5. Custom Audience Retargeting Funnels & Lookalike Seed Lists
* **The 3-Step Retargeting Funnel:**
  * *Step 1 (Cold Traffic):* Run Video Ad to Broad Interest Audience[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
  * *Step 2 (Custom Audience Creation):* Create Custom Audience of 75%+ Video Viewers & Website Visitors[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).
  * *Step 3 (Hot Retargeting Ad):* Show Offer / Testimonial Ad to this Custom Audience for instant conversions[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
* **Lookalike Audience (LAL):** 1,000 verified buyers ko seed data banakar Meta AI ke zariye poore desh mein waise hi 1% new high-converting users find kiye jate hain[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
